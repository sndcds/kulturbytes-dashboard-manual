The data for your organization is divided into different **tabs**. Each tab contains specific information.


## Basic information

This tab contains the most important information about your organization.


### Name

Enter the name of your organization here. Use the **legally correct name** wherever possible. This name may be used in the legal notice, on websites, or in other official information.


### Description

You can provide more information about your organization here and format it using **Markdown**, for example with headings, lists, links, and emphasis.

Keep the description clear and reasonably short. A short and easy-to-understand description is easier for visitors to read.

The description is displayed on your organization's **detail page**.


### Legal form

Select the legal form of your organization here.

If your legal form is not yet available in the list, you can let the **kulturbytes development team** know. We can add it in a future update.


### Website

Enter your organization's website address here.

The website is linked from your organization's information and can also be shown on event and venue detail pages, making it easy for visitors to find more information about your organization.


### Email

Enter your organization's email address here.

Visitors can use this address to contact your organization.


### Phone

Enter your organization's phone number here.


### Street

Enter the name of the street where your organization is located.

### House number

Enter the house number here.


### Address addition

You can enter additional address information here.

For example:

* rear building
* Building B
* 2nd floor


### Postal code

Enter the postal code here.


### City

Enter the city or town where your organization is located.


### Country

Select the country here.

### State

Select the state or federal state where your organization is located.


## Map

## Logos

Here you can upload logo images for your organization. For information about logo formats, recommended dimensions, different logo variants and their uses, see the [Logos](../logos/) page.
